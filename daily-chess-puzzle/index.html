How to play
  • Every day there's one new puzzle — the same for everyone. It changes at midnight.
  • Find the checkmate. Drag a piece or tap it and tap a square.
  • Wrong move? No problem — it's taken back and you can try again as many times as you like.
  • Stuck? Hint first shows which piece to move, then where it goes.
  • In longer puzzles the opponent replies automatically after each of your moves.
